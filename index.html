Hi there 👋

I am a second-year PhD student at The University of Tokyo, supervised by Prof. Yu Chen.

My research focuses on Financial AI, including financial reasoning, AI agents for financial analysis, and information extraction from financial documents.

In 2025, I joined MBZUAI as a visiting student in NLP, where I worked with Prof. Preslav Nakov and Dr. Zhuohan Xie. Before that, I was also a visiting student at NUS AIDF, hosted by Prof. Kewei Huang.

Before starting my PhD, I worked as a quantitative researcher in industry, focusing on the localization of Barra-style multi-factor risk models, portfolio risk forecasting, and statistical modeling of factor covariance matrices.

That experience shaped how I think about research. Quantitative finance is, above all, about working with data. In industry, the devil is often in the details. Progress rarely comes from using the latest or most sophisticated algorithms; it comes from understanding the data, questioning assumptions, and solving countless small problems that determine whether a model works in practice.

Today, I hope to bring this mindset into Financial AI. My goal is to bridge rigorous quantitative modeling with modern AI, connecting academic research with real-world financial practice.

Cold head, Warm heart.

Open to collaborations and opportunities from both academia and industry.
